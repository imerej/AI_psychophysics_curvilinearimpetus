Moving ball study

You will watch a small red ball move through a tube lying flat on a table, seen from above, and say what happens next. It takes about 12 minutes. There are no trick questions: answer with your first impression.

No personal data is collected beyond the answers below. Your answers are saved to a file on this computer at the end.

Part 1 of 2 · Draw the path

Each time, a ball goes through a curved (or straight) tube lying flat on a table. You see either the ball moving through the tube, or a single picture. Both stop just before the ball comes out.

Then draw the path the ball will follow when it comes out of the tube: press on the ball and drag, as far as the edge of the picture. Nothing pushes or pulls the ball once it is out. Use Redo to try again, then Next.

30 drawings.

Part 2 of 2 · Which looks natural?

Now each tube is shown twice, as version 1 and version 2. They are the same until the ball comes out of the tube, then the ball moves differently.

Choose the version in which the ball moves the way a real ball would. You can replay them.

30 choices.
